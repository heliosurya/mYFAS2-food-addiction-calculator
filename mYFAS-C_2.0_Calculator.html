mYFAS-C 2.0 for Children (Brief)

A brief, 13-item version of the YFAS-C 2.0 that assesses food addiction symptoms in children. Adapted from Horsager & Gearhardt (2024) — UMich FASTlab.

0
Symptom Count (0-11)
No Food Addiction
Diagnosis
1. I've lost control over when or how much I eat
2. I've spent a lot of time eating or thinking about food
3. I've kept eating even after my parents said I should stop
4. I've had so much to eat that I felt sick
5. I've wanted to cut down on certain foods but couldn't
6. I've gotten really mad or upset when I couldn't have certain foods
7. I've kept eating certain foods even when I didn't like how I felt afterward
8. I've noticed that I need more and more of certain foods to feel satisfied
9. I've had physical problems like stomach aches or headaches from eating certain foods
10. When I cut down or stop eating certain foods, I feel withdrawal symptoms
11. I've continued to eat certain foods even though I know it's causing problems in my life
12. I've had a strong urge or craving to eat certain foods
13. I've had a hard time cutting down on how much I eat, and it's made things worse at home, school, or with friends
