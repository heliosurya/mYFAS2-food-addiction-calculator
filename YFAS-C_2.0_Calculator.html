Diagnosis
1. I've lost control over when or how much I eat (like I can't stop or stop myself)
2. I've spent a lot of time eating or thinking about food
3. I've kept eating even after my parents said I should stop
4. I've had so much to eat that I felt sick
5. I've felt bad because I couldn't eat the foods I wanted
6. I've wanted to cut down on certain foods but couldn't
7. I've gotten really mad or upset when I couldn't have certain foods
8. I've kept eating certain foods even when I didn't like how I felt afterward
9. I've noticed that I need more and more of certain foods to feel satisfied
10. I've had physical problems like stomach aches or headaches from eating certain foods
11. When I cut down or stop eating certain foods, I feel withdrawal symptoms (like getting upset, having a hard time paying attention, or feeling sick)
12. I've spent a lot of time doing things that make me think about or want to eat certain foods
13. I've continued to eat certain foods even though I know it's causing problems in my life (like at home, school, or with friends)
14. I've had a hard time cutting down or controlling how much I eat, and it's made things worse at home, school, or with friends
15. I've had a big mood change or felt upset when I couldn't eat certain foods
16. I've noticed that certain foods are more rewarding to eat than other foods
17. I've spent a lot of time and effort getting certain foods, using them, or recovering from their effects
18. I've had a strong urge or craving to eat certain foods
