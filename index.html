# Symptom criterion Item Your score Threshold Met?
How scoring works. Each of the 11 DSM-5 symptom criteria is scored 0 or 1 based on whether the item frequency meets the criterion's threshold (≥2 once/month, ≥4 once/week, or ≥5 two–three times/week). Clinical significance is met if item 5 or 6 scores ≥5. Severity: No Food Addiction (≤1 symptom, or >1 symptom without clinical significance) · Mild (2–3 + sig) · Moderate (4–5 + sig) · Severe (6+ + sig). Thresholds follow the mYFAS 2.0 scoring instructions (Schulte & Gearhardt, Eur Eat Disord Rev, 2017).
